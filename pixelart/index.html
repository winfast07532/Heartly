A MOMENT FOR YOU

Find your flow.

Pick a numbered color, then paint its matching squares. Drag across them to keep coloring.

  • Zoom in with two fingers, the + button, or your scroll wheel.
  • Move with two fingers, or drag from a colored square, a different number, or outside the picture.
  • Fill costs 100 points per connected group of matching squares. Select Fill, then tap a matching square. Each use returns to free Paint.
  • Find a cell costs 50 points and takes you to the next square for your color, then returns to free Paint.

Progress saves automatically. Each picture earns Heartly points once. Replays earn no extra points; paid tools still cost points. Developer previews are free.
